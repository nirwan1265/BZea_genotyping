BZea BC2S3 introgressions (k-mer HMM, round 0)

Pick a taxon and a family. "All pools" shows every pool of the family on each chromosome, so shared segments line up.

B73: all plants AA SEG: family segregates, plants AA, AB or BB (1:2:1) DONOR: all plants BB

Each sample is a pool of 6 BC2S3 plants from one family. States are pool states from the k-mer HMM (steps 20–21), in 100-kb windows. SEG is not "het": in a SEG region the plants of the pool are ¼ AA, ½ AB and ¼ BB. Donor share = DONOR + SEG/2. NOISY pools (σ > 0.15) are low-depth and not trusted.
